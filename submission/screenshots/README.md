## Best Practices for Taking Screenshots

1. **Resolution:** Capture at standard 1080p (`1920x1080`) or clean retina resolution for crisp text.
2. **Format:** Save in PNG format (`.png`) for optimal quality without compression artifacts.
3. **Browser Zoom:** Ensure your browser zoom is at 100% (`Ctrl + 0`).
4. **Cropping:** Capture the entire viewport or the active modal cleanly without cutting off card borders or buttons.
